# Theme Glyph Families

Each visual theme owns five premium-square glyphs. The glyphs are presentation only; multiplier rules remain in the selected ruleset.

| Key | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `d` | Double letter |
| `t` | Triple letter |
| `D` | Double word |
| `T` | Triple word |
| `*` | Center/start |

Production requirements:

- Readable between 30 and 68 pixels.
- Transparent outside the emblem; no baked tile or backdrop.
- Broad silhouette, restrained interior detail, and strong value contrast.
- Only the five atlas positions are shared. Themes may use entirely different objects, silhouettes, compositions, and visual grammar.
- No letters, numerals, multiplier text, trademarks, or copyrighted insignia.
- A covered premium square shows only the placed letter tile.
- Tooltip and hover reveal remain the accessible source of the exact bonus name and multiplier.

Theme motifs:

- Wizardry: engraved brass and amethyst celestial seals.
- Gothic Horror: five haunted architectural and devotional artifacts; black iron, oxidized silver, thorn, moon, rose, and cathedral glass.
- Pirate: five working captain's-cabin objects; compass, rope, tide, navigational tools, sailcloth, and weathered brass.
- Space Age: five distinct pieces of optimistic starship instrumentation; luminous cyan glass, ivory alloy, orbital diagrams, and stellar energy.
- Kitchen Witchery: five handmade household charms; copper, green enamel, herbs, spoon, cauldron, hearth, and protective rosette.
- Prairie Homestead: five practical folk-art objects; carved wood, wheat, stitchwork, quilt geometry, horseshoe, and barn star.
- Velvet & Leather: five luxurious talismans; antique gold, blackened steel, ribbon, ring, rose, lattice, and faceted jewel.

Generate and approve one complete family at a time. Wizardry establishes the atlas composition and runtime treatment before the other six are produced.
